Round 9: keywords and types. 1 is the current theme: a saturated sky blue on keywords, and the palest, brightest yellow on every type name, so types out-shout everything. A muted, grown-up mid blue turned out to collide with the pink functions for red-blind readers: to them the pink looks like a greyish periwinkle (#a4a4dd). So the new blues are pale and icy: periwinkle leans violet, steel is grey-blue, cornflower is the bluest. Types move to a calm sea green, so yellow is used only for warnings. A darker gold for types was tried and dropped: color-blind readers couldn't tell it from the orange numbers. Constants go back to a saturated violet, because the pale lavender looked like the icy keywords. Each card runs the color-blind check. Pick a number.
